HOW TO Play

TouchDrag anywhere to steer. Hold BOOST, or touch with a second finger.
MouseYour snake follows the pointer. Hold the button or Space to boost.
KeysArrows or WASD to turn. Shift to boost.
ORBSEvery orb you swallow makes you longer. Level up every few dozen.
BODYRun your head into another snake's body and you are eaten, whatever your size. Cut the big ones off.
HEAD ONHead to head, the snake at least 20% bigger wins. Any closer and you both go.
DROPSA dead snake spills its whole length as orbs. Kills are a feast, but so is being nearby.
BOOSTBoosting is fast but burns length and sheds it behind you. Small snakes can't afford much.
MUTATEEvery five levels, pick one of three mutations. They stack, and they last the run.
PICKUPSRare glowing diamonds, and the bots want them too. G Ghost passes through bodies for three seconds. S Shrink Ray takes 30% off the nearest bigger snake. O Overdrive is five seconds of free boost. D Decoy drops a mine behind you that eats the next head to touch it.
BOSSEach tier ends with a boss, at a level or after four minutes. Bosses steer round a tail they see coming, so cut close or bait the lunge. Ouroboros coils and lunges. Hydra grows a new snake from every cut. Basilisk fires a beam down a line it shows you first, and its body is safe to cross while it aims. Wyrm phases out and returns beside you. Lay your tail across a boss's path: its head into your body costs it a hit. Head on, it always wins.
WALLThe pink edge is solid. You slide along it, but it is a fine place to get cornered. In Endless it closes in every minute.
DAILYOne seeded arena a day, one attempt, the same for everyone. Three missions a day pay coins.
