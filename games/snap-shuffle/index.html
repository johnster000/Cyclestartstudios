HOW TO Play

A picture is cut into square tiles and shuffled. Put every tile back where it came from and the picture comes back together.

Drag a tile onto another and the two switch places. When a tile lands next to its true neighbour — the piece of the picture that really sits beside it — the border between them melts away and they become one piece that moves together.

LOOSE TILE JOINED PIECE BIG PIECE NEARLY THERE

Whatever you land on jumps back into the space you left — even out of another piece. That is how you take a piece apart, so nothing is ever stuck. You can also tap a tile, then tap where it should go.

Stuck? Hold PEEK to see the finished picture. Peeks are free, but a solve with none, under par time, earns three stars.

Pictures come from Lorem Picsum — landscapes, skies, streets and textures — and a new one is dealt for every challenge. With no connection the game paints its own abstract pictures instead.
