HOW TO Play

The pink car is yours, and it is boxed in. Get it to the exit on the right edge of its lane.

Drag any vehicle along its lane. Cars slide forwards and backwards only — never sideways — and never through each other. You can also tap a vehicle, then tap the square you want it to reach.

YOUR CAR CAR · 2 LONG TRUCK · 3 LONG EXIT

Sliding one vehicle any distance is one move. Every level has a par: clear the jam in par or fewer, with no hints, for three stars. Match the fewest moves possible and it is a perfect clear.

Stuck? HINT shows the next move of a best line — it costs one from your stock and a star. UNDO is free and unlimited. Every level you clear for the first time earns a hint back.
