HOW TO Play

Every arrow is boxed in by the others. An arrow can leave when its lane is clear: the straight line from its head to the edge of the board, with no other arrow standing anywhere on it. The open air around a shape never blocks.

Tap a free arrow and it slides out along its own path, freeing the way for others. Tap a blocked one and you lose a heart; lose them all and the level is over. Nothing you do can ever trap an arrow for good — every board can always be cleared.

FREE · LANE CLEAR BLOCKED · SOMEONE IN THE LANE HEARTS · YOUR MISSES

Clear a level with no misses for three stars. A HINT lights up a free arrow; it costs one from your stock and a star. Every level you clear for the first time earns a hint back. Pinch or scroll to zoom in on the big ones.
